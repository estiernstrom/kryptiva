Services

Whatever the size of the job, you get help from specialists who explain what needs to be done and why.

For businesses and organisations

  • Security audits

    We review systems, processes and routines, and show where the risks are and what to fix first.

  • GRC: governance, risk and compliance

    Support with policies, risk assessments and the requirements that laws and standards place on your organisation.

  • Penetration testing

    We test your systems the way an attacker would and report the weaknesses with clear recommendations.

  • Advisory and consulting

    Security expertise when you need it, for a single question or as support through an entire project.

  • Encryption

    Advice on protecting data at rest and in transit, and on choosing and introducing the right solutions.

  • Privacy and data protection

    Help handling personal data and sensitive information in a way that protects both customers and the business.

For individuals

  • Router, network and internet

    We install and configure your router and home network so the connection is both stable and secure.

  • Secure devices and accounts

    A review of computers, phones and accounts, with help getting started with strong passwords and two-step verification.

  • Privacy online

    Advice on protecting your personal information and reducing the traces you leave behind.

How an assignment works

The approach is the same whether it concerns a home network or an audit of an entire organisation.

  1. We listen

    You tell us what you need help with. We ask questions until we understand the situation and what matters most to you.

  2. We propose

    You get a clear proposal for what we will do, how long it takes and what it costs.

  3. We deliver

    We do the work and keep you updated along the way.

  4. We follow up

    You get the result explained in plain language, with concrete next steps.

About us

Kryptiva is run by Jonna and Erik, two cybersecurity specialists who are married and run the company together. We started Kryptiva to make qualified security help available to everyone, whether you are a large company or a private individual.

We work closely with our clients, explain things without unnecessary jargon, and adapt the work to what is actually needed.
