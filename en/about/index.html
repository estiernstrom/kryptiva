About us

Kryptiva is run by Jonna and Erik, two cybersecurity specialists who are married and run the company together. We started Kryptiva to make qualified security help available to everyone, whether you are a large company or a private individual.

We work closely with our clients, explain things without unnecessary jargon, and adapt the work to what is actually needed.

  • J

    Jonna

    Cybersecurity specialist and co-founder

    Jonna has worked as a cybersecurity specialist at Sundsvall Municipality and knows how security work functions in a large public organisation, where requirements, daily operations and limited resources have to fit together.

    She holds a university diploma in systems science from Mid Sweden University.

  • E

    Erik

    Cybersecurity specialist and co-founder

    Erik combines a background in systems science with specialist study in cybersecurity, so he understands both how systems are built and how they are attacked.

    He holds a master's degree in cybersecurity from Luleå University of Technology.

    Erik was a finalist in Prompt-SM 2025, a Swedish prompting competition, where he finished fourth. The final was held at Google.
